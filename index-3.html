Get to know Taniti

More than a beach destination

Taniti brings together sandy and rocky beaches, a safe harbor, rainforest, and a mountainous interior with a small active volcano. Fishing and agriculture have traditionally shaped the island’s economy.

✈️ Air travel

A small airport serves small jets and propeller planes. Expansion for larger jets is planned in coming years.

🚌 Public buses

Taniti City buses run daily from 5am to 11pm. Private buses serve the rest of the island.

🚕 Taxis and rentals

Taxis operate in Taniti City. Rental cars are available near the airport.

🚲 Bikes and walking

Bikes and helmets are available from several vendors; helmets are legally required. Taniti City is flat and walkable, and Merriton Landing is easy to explore on foot.

Planning to visit Merriton Landing?

The area is developing on the north side of Yellow Leaf Bay and is easy to explore on foot.

Grocery options

Taniti has two supermarkets, two smaller grocery stores, and one 24-hour convenience store.

Alcohol service hours

Alcohol is not served or sold from midnight to 9am. The drinking age is 18, though the island information notes that the age rule is not strictly enforced.

Helpful visitor information

Frequently asked questions

Answers below are based on the supplied “About the Island” information. Check locally for current rules and conditions.

What currency is used?

The U.S. dollar is the island’s currency. Many businesses accept euros and yen; banks exchange currency, and major credit cards are accepted.

What electrical outlets are used?

Electrical outlets are 120 volts, the same as in the United States.

What languages are spoken?

Many younger Tanitians speak English fluently. English is less commonly spoken in rural areas, especially among older residents.

Is healthcare available?

Taniti has one hospital and several clinics. The hospital has multilingual employees.

Is Taniti safe for visitors?

Violent crime is rare, but reports of pickpocketing and other petty crime have increased with tourism. Keep valuables secure and stay aware of your surroundings.

Do holidays affect opening hours?

Yes. National holidays may cause attractions and restaurants to close.

When can alcohol be sold or served?

Alcohol is not sold or served between midnight and 9am. The stated drinking age is 18.

How often do cruise ships arrive?

Some visitors arrive by cruise ship; the supplied island information says a cruise ship docks in Yellow Leaf Bay one night per week.
